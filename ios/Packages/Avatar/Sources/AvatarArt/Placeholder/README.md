# Placeholder character

Stand-in art and motion until the character design is decided. The formats are the real thing.

- `rig.json`: the parts, back to front, each with its parent, pivot and art layers. Every part is drawn on the shared 240×320 canvas in its rest pose, so artists export each layer from one artboard. `defaults` names the variant a slot wears when a look leaves it out: round eyes and a smile, the face every animal keeps, and 小白鞋 (`sneaker`).
- `art/base/<art>.svg`: parts every character shares (the shadow). `art/<slot>/<variant>/<art>.svg`: what a look picks per slot: `body` (`peach` for people, whose skin tone is a swatch; species and coat for animals: cats, dogs, bears, rabbits), `hair`, the face (`eyes`, `brows`, `mouth`, `cheeks`, `glasses`), `hat`, `top`, `bottom`, `shoes`, `prop`. Bodies draw bare legs; every species wears the `shoes`, drawn over the legs and bottoms. Each mouth draws `closed` and `open`, which the cheers swap between. People pick hair and a face (`editor.json` says which species are people); animals' faces come with their coat. A variant may skip a part (people and bears draw no `tail`). Adding a variant is adding its folder: `PlaceholderCharacter.variants(for:)` lists what's bundled, and the tests run every variant through every persona's clips. Nothing may leave the canvas mid-jump, which is why rabbit ears droop to the sides instead of standing up. Animals wear hats but no hair.
- `art/<slot>/<variant>/tones.json`: the colors a look may recolor in that variant, `{"version": 1, "base": "#2D5BA0", "shades": ["#28467E"]}`. `base` is the main color; `shades` are the outlines, folds and stripes drawn from it, which keep their relation to it (a darker outline stays as much darker). Every other color in the art (blush, highlights, trim) keeps its own. Hair, eyes, brows, top and bottom variants have one, and so do bodies with a plain coat; a patterned coat (a calico, a husky) keeps its colors and has none, as do hats, shoes and props. `PlaceholderCharacter.swatches(for:wearing:)` lists the preset colors the editor offers: all from the design system's palette: its scene and clothes colors for clothes, its character tones for skin, fur, hair and eyes. Brows offer the hair colors and follow the hair (the editor's `CharacterDraft`) until given their own.
- `editor.json`: what the character editor shows beyond every bundled variant: bodies grouped by species in display order (`person` shows only `peach` and takes skin tones from the swatches; `"hair": true` marks the species that wears hair), and the hats, shoes and props of this release. Bundled art the lists leave out stays hidden, such as the swim cap only the swim kit wears. The first shoes are the rig's default.
- `kits.json`: what a character wears for a sport, by kit (`wear`: a variant per slot, `null` takes it off, slots left out stay as worn), and which kit each `Sport` puts on: its own entry under `sports`, else its category's under `categories`, else `otherwise` (`warmup`, which changes nothing). A kit keeps body, hair, face and colors (`SportKit.dress`). Picking a sport plays `moves/kit-out.json` (a quick squash), swaps the kit in, plays `moves/kit-in.json` (a pop up and a landing), then loops the kit's move `moves/sports/<kit>.json` at the persona's `accentTempo` (`CharacterPlayer.change`). Taking a sport off has no move: the screen dresses the character back with `wear`. No clip turns, flips or spins the whole character: the art is flat, so it reads badly side-on or mirrored (a test checks every clip).
- `art/desk/laptop/desk.svg` and `moves/typing.json`: 我's character at a small desk behind its laptop (`PlaceholderCharacter.atDesk`, which also takes the hand prop off), the desk the rig's last part so it stands in front of everything and stays put while the character moves. Typing loops a quick bob as the hands tap, and rises up now and then to peek over the screen (`PlaceholderCharacter.typing(persona:)`, at the persona's `accentTempo`).
- `moves/drop.json`: the squad dropping into a newly revealed scene (`PlaceholderCharacter.drop()`), the same for every look and persona: falls in from 420 points above its spot with the shadow growing under it, squashes on touch-down at `dropLands` while dust (`dustL`, `dustR`, hidden at rest) puffs out from both feet, bounces once, wobbles and lands at rest. Like the entrance it starts off the canvas, so the screen draws it with a `crop` above the stage and plays it without blending.
- `moves/teary.json`: about to cry, for 注销账号 (`PlaceholderCharacter.teary(persona:)`): it hides the brows and the smile and shows the rig's sad face for the whole loop: each brows variant's `sad.svg` (the same brows, worried), the shared `art/base/frown.svg`, and `tearL`/`tearR`, which well up from the lower lid, roll down the cheek and fade, one after the other, then a sniffle. All of them rest hidden (opacity 0), so every look and persona can cry; looks without brows cry without them.
- `moves/entrance.json`: 上场, the same for every look and persona: runs in from the right, skids, crouches, star-jumps and lands squashed on its spot, at rest. The one clip that leaves the canvas: the root carries the figure across the stage and up (the shadow follows sideways only), so the screen draws it with a `crop` that covers the stage.
- `clips/<persona>/idle.json`, `cheer.json` and `welcome.json`: each persona's idle loop, check-in cheer and 上场 welcome (teammates cheering a new member in, and the newcomer on landing), as keyframe tracks per part and channel (`rotation` in degrees clockwise, `x`, `y`, `scaleX`, `scaleY`, `opacity`). A key's `ease` shapes the move into it: `linear`, `hold`, `easeIn`, `easeOut`, `easeInOut`, `backOut` or four cubic-Bézier numbers.
- `accents/<species>/idle.json` and `cheer.json`: each species' own moves, played on top of whichever persona's clip (`PlaceholderCharacter.clip(_:persona:species:)`, by the species id `editor.json` groups a body under): a cat's yawn and tail flick, a dog's wag and head tilt, a rabbit's nose and ears, a bear's belly scratch and butt wiggle, people's uneven blinks and brow raise. Same format as a clip, but values are offsets: `rotation`, `x`, `y` and `opacity` add to the persona's, scales multiply it, and every track starts and ends at rest so nothing jumps where clips loop or hand over. An idle accent loops on its own length; a cheer accent is stretched over the persona's cheer. Each persona plays them at its own tempo and strength (`Persona.accentTempo`, `accentStrength`): a deadpan cat yawns slowly, an energetic dog wags fast. Ears that move are parts of their own: upright ones (`earL`, `earR`) behind the head, a lop's floppy ones (`flapL`, `flapR`) over it, then `crown` over those for the hat and anything the coat draws over the ears (a lop's shine and blush); a rabbit's `nose` too. Ears that don't move (a golden's, a bichon's) stay in the head's art.
- Personas: `deadpan`, `energetic`, `showoff`, `drama`, `shy`. Every look plays every persona. Adding one is a new `clips/<name>/` folder with its three clips, plus a `Persona` case of that name; the tests run over every case and check it binds, loops seamlessly, lands where its idle starts and stays on the canvas.
- SVG: flat paths, circles, ellipses and rects with hex fills and strokes. No transforms, gradients, filters, text or arcs (outline them).
