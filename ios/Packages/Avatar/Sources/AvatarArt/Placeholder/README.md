# Placeholder character

Stand-in art and motion until the character design is decided. The formats are the real thing.

- `rig.json`: the parts, back to front, each with its parent, pivot and art layers. Every part is drawn on the shared 240×320 canvas in its rest pose, so artists export each layer from one artboard. `defaults` names the variant a slot wears when a look leaves it out: round eyes and a smile, and WARM-UP's tee, shorts and canvas shoes. A look whose piece this build has no art for (retired, or released later) wears the default there instead (`PlaceholderCharacter.drawable`).
- `art/base/<art>.svg`: parts every character shares (the shadow). `art/<slot>/<variant>/<art>.svg`: what a look picks per slot: `body` (`peach` for people, whose skin tone is a swatch; species and coat for animals: cats, dogs, bears, rabbits), `hair`, the face (`eyes`, `brows`, `mouth`, `cheeks`, `glasses`), `hat`, `top`, `bottom`, `shoes`, `acc`, `prop`. Clothing comes from six fictional stores, each piece named `<store>-<piece>` (`warmup-tee`, `lap-jacket`). A top draws `torso`, and may add `over` (a hem drawn over the bottom's waistband, for jackets and jerseys), and sleeves on `armL`/`armR`. A bottom worn over the top (the overalls) may add `over` too, drawn after the top's, so a jacket's hem goes under the bib instead of across it; it repeats the seat, which older builds draw from `torso`. Sleeves are filled tubes with a flat cuff the arm comes out of, over a disc of the sleeve's color on the arm's pivot that covers the round shoulder the body's arm starts in, which stays put while the arm turns (otherwise it shows under a raised sleeve as a hole of skin); tanks cut a round armhole around the arm's root instead of painting over it. A sleeve keeps its cloth to the arm (inside the shoulder's disc and a band along the arm that widens toward the elbow, then the whole sleeve to the cuff; `everySleeveKeepsToTheArm`), since cloth reaching in onto the chest would turn with the arm and cover a strap or bag there. The shoulder is the top's own colour with no seam drawn; only a raglan top (a shoulder in another colour) draws that panel on its `torso`, as one clean shape, where it stays put under what is worn. An accessory (`acc`: a sling bag, a towel, a jacket tied at the waist) draws `torso`, over the top and under the arms, its strap too, so a hand brought up to the chest is never covered by it. Each arm is drawn once, whole, always in front of the body and everything worn on it, and under the head, as in any 2D rig: an arm never changes depth, so it never jumps from behind the body to in front of it and is never cut at the elbow. The head always covers the arms, so no arm or sleeve ever crosses the face. An idle's arms hang a little out from the sides (turned 12° and moved 3 points outward, which the cheers and welcomes start and end on). Every top with long sleeves draws the round shoulder its sleeves start in on its `torso` too (a disc of the sleeve's colour on each arm's pivot, first in the file so the shirt's own pattern stays over it), so the shoulder stays whole while the arm moves out. A move uploaded for older builds may still carry a `front` track (which picked an arm's depth before); this build leaves it out. A hand never goes on the face: a move that brings the hands to the face, the cheeks or above the head holds them at the head's edge, where they peek out beside it. The brows draw under the hat, so a brim covers them rather than wearing them. A bichon's fluff hangs below a person's chin, so it draws `chin`, that fluff again, over the arms like the head. A bottom's `torso` (the seat) starts with a triangle of its darkest shade in the notch between the legs, so the top under it never shows through there. Bodies draw bare legs; every species wears the `shoes`, drawn over the legs and bottoms. Each mouth draws `closed` and `open`, which the cheers swap between. People pick hair (`editor.json` says which species are people), and everyone picks a face. A coat draws no blush, so cheeks are the face's to pick, and an animal starts on `rosy` (`PlaceholderCharacter.animalCheeks`); only floppy-eared coats keep their own blush clear of the ears (a golden's in its head, a lop's in its `shine`) and start without cheeks (`PlaceholderCharacter.blushingCoats`). A variant may skip a part (people and bears draw no `tail`). Adding a variant is adding its folder: `PlaceholderCharacter.variants(for:)` lists what's bundled, and the tests run every variant through every persona's clips. Nothing may leave the canvas mid-jump, which is why rabbit ears droop to the sides instead of standing up. Animals wear hats but no hair.
- `art/<slot>/<variant>/tones.json`: the colors a look may recolor in that variant, `{"version": 1, "base": "#2D5BA0", "shades": ["#28467E"]}`. `base` is the main color; `shades` are the outlines, folds and stripes drawn from it, which keep their relation to it (a darker outline stays as much darker). Every other color in the art (blush, highlights, trim) keeps its own. Hair, eyes, brows and most clothes have one, and so do bodies with a plain coat; a patterned coat (a calico, a husky) keeps its colors and has none, as do props and clothes whose colors are the point (LAP NINE's runners, NO LOOK's cap). `PlaceholderCharacter.swatches(for:wearing:)` lists the preset colors the editor offers besides the art's own: the design system's character tones for skin, fur, hair and eyes, and each piece of clothing's own colors from `editor.json`. Brows offer the hair colors and follow the hair (the editor's `CharacterDraft`) until given their own.
- `editor.json`: what the character editor shows beyond every bundled variant: bodies grouped by species in display order (`person` shows only `peach` and takes skin tones from the swatches; `"hair": true` marks the species that wears hair), the shopping street's `stores` in display order, the clothes on their racks under `wear`, by slot (`top`, `bottom`, `shoes`, `hat`, `acc`), each with the store it hangs in and its preset colors, the art's own first (WARM-UP's twelve pieces, and the other stores' tops and bottoms in their store's colors; their shoes, hats and accessories keep their art's colors), and the props of this release. A store or prop marked `"open": true` is every member's from the start (WARM-UP 热身社 and every prop, Tracy 2026-11-09); the other stores open once the member unlocks them by training, by the API's rules (`GET /me/unlocks`). Bundled art the lists leave out stays hidden, such as a piece only the website's cast wears. `stores/<id>.svg` is each store's badge (28 × 28), drawn on its card on the street.
- `names.json`: every bundled store's words in three languages (its sign, its name in the reader's language, its sports, its line, how it unlocks, a limited one's tag and the tag's colour), `{"version": 1, "stores": {…}, "variants": {…}}`. An uploaded `avatar/names.json` stands in front of it word by word (`ArtNames.overlaid(by:)`), so a store is renamed or added with `put_art`, never in code; a variant named there stands in front of the String Catalog's name.
- `kits.json`: what a character trains with for a sport, by kit (`prop`: the prop it hands over; none keeps the one in hand), and which kit each `Sport` takes: its own entry under `sports`, else its category's under `categories`, else `otherwise` (`warmup`, which changes nothing). A kit never changes clothes, body, hair, face or colors, so a character wears the look its member saved on every screen (`SportKit.dress`). Picking a sport plays `moves/kit-out.json` (a quick squash), hands the prop over, plays `moves/kit-in.json` (a pop up and a landing), then loops the kit's move at the persona's `accentTempo`: its own `moves/sports/<kit>.json` where it has one (the run kit's jog, while it carries the tumbler, and the warm-up), else the signature move of the prop it holds (`moves/props/<prop>.json`) (`CharacterPlayer.change`). Taking a sport off has no move: the screen goes back to the saved look. No clip turns, flips or spins the whole character: the art is flat, so it reads badly side-on or mirrored (a test checks every clip).
- `art/desk/laptop/desk.svg` and `moves/typing.json`: 我's character at a small desk behind its laptop (`PlaceholderCharacter.atDesk`, which also takes the hand prop off), the desk the rig's last part so it stands in front of everything and stays put while the character moves. Typing loops a quick bob as the hands tap, and rises up now and then to peek over the screen (`PlaceholderCharacter.typing(persona:)`, at the persona's `accentTempo`).
- `art/desk/notebook/desk.svg`, `art/prop/pencil/` and `moves/writing.json`: 意见反馈's character at the same desk writing in an open notebook, a pencil in its right hand (`PlaceholderCharacter.atDeskWriting`). Writing loops a scribble of the pencil hand with the head bowed over the page, then a pause to think, pencil raised and eyes up (`PlaceholderCharacter.writing(persona:)`, at the persona's `accentTempo`). The pencil is only for this; no store or editor lists it.
- `moves/props/<prop>.json`: a held prop's own move, played once in the character editor whenever the look changes, and again after 12 idle seconds on its 性格 tab (`PlaceholderCharacter.propMove(for:)`), each the move someone who does that sport knows at once: the racket's toss-and-serve, the badminton jump smash, the pickleball dink, the ping pong serve and fist pump, the dumbbell's press and flex, the kettlebell goblet squat, the yoga mat rolled out for a tree pose, the chalk bag dip and clap, the goggles snapped on for a breaststroke (the water is `propOver`, a see-through band that rises from the bottom), the tumbler lifted to the chin for a long sip, the helmet put on to pedal the bike (front view, its wheel `propBack`, the bar `propOver`) and ring its bell, the boxing guard with two jabs and a cross, two dribbles and a jump shot (the ball flies off as `propOver`), foot-knee-foot keepie-uppies caught in both hands, carving on skis with pole plants and a hop, and riding a snowboard over bumps into an ollie. Each says `"holds": "<prop>"`, so it turns its prop itself. A prop without one leaves the persona's cheer to play.
- Elbows: `armL` and `armR` each name an `elbow` in `rig.json` (`at`, and the `hand` that gives the arm's direction). A clip's `bend` track turns the forearm there: the art past the elbow turns with it, the upper arm and a short sleeve stay, and the art between curves (`AvatarDomain`'s `Bend`), so every body and sleeve bends as one soft arm from its one-piece art, and whatever the arm carries (`propR`, `gripR`) moves with the forearm. An arm a clip swings without a `bend` track follows through: the forearm trails the swing by 0.09 s (at most 35°) and straightens when it stops, a one-shot straightening over its last moment, and the prop keeps the angle the clip gave it. Write `bend` only for a pose that bends on purpose, such as a boxer's guard. A flat `bend` of 0 keeps an arm straight instead, as the drop and the shy and deadpan welcomes do for `armR`, whose prop would otherwise trail off the canvas.
- Holding a prop: `propR` (on `armR`) draws after the head, so a prop sits in front of the forearm, and `gripR` (on `propR`) draws the mitten fist over its handle from the prop's `grip.svg`. The fist is drawn in the rig's `skin` (#F5CDAE, outline #B89A8B), and a layer that names its `hand` (`"hand": "armR"`) is repainted to that hand's own colors, the last shape of the body's `armR` art, so one grip serves every species and skin tone. A prop that hangs from the hand rather than being gripped (goggles, the chalk bag, the tumbler, the kettlebell, the rolled yoga mat) has `art/prop/<id>/hold.json`, `{"version": 1, "level": "armR"}`: in every clip but its own move, its parts on that arm turn back by everything that carries them, so it hangs level and stays on the canvas. `propBack` (behind the body) and `propFront` (on the head) are a prop move's extra layers, hidden at rest. A prop for both hands draws `propL` (on `armL`, after the head), with `gripL` for the left fist (the ski poles; the boxing gloves are fists themselves and draw no grip). Gear stood on draws `feet` on `propFeet`, a root part behind the legs that stays on at rest, so skis and a snowboard are under the boots whatever the character does, and skis, which point at the viewer, draw their tips as `toes` on `propToes`, the same over the legs; sitting, holding the 请假条, sleeping and waking put both away with the hand props, and the moves that free the hands (heart, chin-up, pout and the flex) put `propL` away with `propR`.
- `moves/drop.json`: the squad dropping into a newly revealed scene (`PlaceholderCharacter.drop()`), the same for every look and persona: falls in from 420 points above its spot with the shadow growing under it, squashes on touch-down at `dropLands` while dust (`dustL`, `dustR`, hidden at rest) puffs out from both feet, bounces once, wobbles and lands at rest. Like the entrance it starts off the canvas, so the screen draws it with a `crop` above the stage and plays it without blending.
- `moves/<move>.json`: one-shot moves any character plays for a moment in the app (`Move`, `PlaceholderCharacter.clip(for:persona:)`, at the persona's `accentTempo`): stretch, doze, warmup, pump, pose, laugh, poked, fullbelly, bump and wave (a hop with both arms up). Each ends at rest so the idle picks up without a jump. Leaning, kicking and bending over reach past the canvas, up to 40 points to either side and 12 above, so a screen that plays them leaves that much room around the figure (`PlaceholderCharacter.moveReachSideways` and `moveReachAbove`).
- `moves/teary.json`: about to cry, for 注销账号 (`PlaceholderCharacter.teary(persona:)`): it hides the brows and the smile and shows the rig's sad face for the whole loop: each brows variant's `sad.svg` (the same brows, worried), the shared `art/base/frown.svg`, and `tearL`/`tearR`, which well up from the lower lid, roll down the cheek and fade, one after the other, then a sniffle. All of them rest hidden (opacity 0), so every look and persona can cry; looks without brows cry without them.
- `moves/sit.json`, `moves/sign.json` and `moves/sleep.json`: the home scene's loops (and 动态's, for a 请假条's author), at the persona's `accentTempo`. Sitting on a rest day (`PlaceholderCharacter.sitting(persona:)`) lowers the body 14 points with the legs out to the sides and breathes; holding the 请假条 (`holdingSign(persona:)`) brings both hands out to the lower corners of the card the scene draws over the chest; asleep at night (`sleeping(persona:)`) sits the same way with the head dropped, eyes shut and the smile hidden. All three put the hand prop away (`propR` at opacity 0). `moves/woken.json` is the sleeper woken by the lights (开灯, `woken()`), played once from `sleep.json`'s pose: a jolt, rubbing an eye, a glare with `browsMad`, `vein` and `mouthSad`, a huff, then standing up slowly to rest; switching the lights off plays `doze.json` back into sleep.
- `moves/reactions/<move>.json` and `moves/reactions/<persona>/<move>.json`: what a post's author plays when a teammate reacts on 动态, by the move id the feed picks for the emoji: `heart` (a big heart pops out at its chest), `flex` (秀肌肉: a double-biceps pose, the arms pumping three times), `chin-up` (得意: hands on hips, nose up, a smirk, a ✨), `laugh-bend` (bends over laughing, tears squirting), `drool` (star eyes, a drool drop, a wipe), `shocked` (a red ! and shock lines), `pout` (V brows, a throbbing 💢, two stomps with steam). Any other emoji plays the persona's own cheer. Each persona has its own take from start to end (`PlaceholderCharacter.reactionMove(_:persona:)`, at its `accentTempo`): 淡人 reacts late, barely moves its body, eyes half shut, then nods; 高能量 bounces right through it and hops twice more; 显眼包 sways to the camera and ends on a pose; 戏精 gasps with arms flung wide, plays it in slow motion and bows; 社恐 glances around, plays it small while edging away and blushing, then hides its face. Each starts and ends at rest, so it crossfades in and out of idle; while the hands are busy (heart, flex, chin-up, pout) the hand prop fades out. The symbols are rig parts that rest hidden (opacity 0, scale 1), most in `art/base/`: `mouthGasp`, `drool`, `puff`, `vein`, `steamL`/`steamR`, `stars`, `gloom`, `bang`, `squirtL`/`squirtR`, `heart`, `heartL`/`heartR`, `sparkle`; `browsMad` is each brows variant's `mad.svg` (looks without brows go without), and 秀肌肉 never swaps the arm for another drawing: the arm itself curls out to the side (rotation, `bend`, a little `scaleY`).
- `art/medal/gold/medal.svg` and `art/crown/gold/award.svg`: the Sunday 战报's gold medal (part `medal`, on the torso, drawn right after it and before the arms) and award crown (part `award`, on the head, drawn right after `crown` so it sits over any hat). Every look wears both by the rig's defaults, hidden (opacity 0); the story shows them by animating their opacity, and the crown's `y` for its drop. The medal's ribbon follows the `medal` colour (`tones.json`), so the story sets it to the scene's accent; the gold stays gold.
- `moves/interactions/`: teammates doing something together (`Interaction`: each one's clip, where they stand and when they start, as designed). 撞头 with a 社恐 (`bumpDodged()`: `bump-miss.json`, `dodge.json`), 抛高高 (`toss()`: `toss-thrower.json` on both sides, `toss-flyer.json`) and 叠罗汉 (`stack()`: `stack-top.json`, which ends `stackTopLandsAt` to the right, and `stack-bottom.json`). They leave the canvas, so they're drawn with a crop around the group. No one turns.
- `moves/entrance.json`: 上场, the same for every look and persona: runs in from the right, skids, crouches, star-jumps and lands squashed on its spot, at rest. The one clip that leaves the canvas: the root carries the figure across the stage and up (the shadow follows sideways only), so the screen draws it with a `crop` that covers the stage.
- `clips/<persona>/idle.json`, `cheer.json` and `welcome.json`: each persona's idle loop, check-in cheer and 上场 welcome (teammates cheering a new member in, and the newcomer on landing), as keyframe tracks per part and channel (`rotation` in degrees clockwise, `x`, `y`, `scaleX`, `scaleY`, `opacity`, and `bend` in degrees clockwise at an arm's elbow). A key's `ease` shapes the move into it: `linear`, `hold`, `easeIn`, `easeOut`, `easeInOut`, `backOut` or four cubic-Bézier numbers.
- `accents/<species>/idle.json` and `cheer.json`: each species' own moves, played on top of whichever persona's clip (`PlaceholderCharacter.clip(_:persona:species:)`, by the species id `editor.json` groups a body under): a cat's yawn and tail flick, a dog's wag and head tilt, a rabbit's nose and ears, a bear's sleepy head tilt and butt wiggle, people's uneven blinks and brow raise. Same format as a clip, but values are offsets: `rotation`, `x`, `y` and `opacity` add to the persona's, scales multiply it, and every track starts and ends at rest so nothing jumps where clips loop or hand over. An idle accent loops on its own length; a cheer accent is stretched over the persona's cheer. Each persona plays them at its own tempo and strength (`Persona.accentTempo`, `accentStrength`): a deadpan cat yawns slowly, an energetic dog wags fast. Ears that move are parts of their own: upright ones (`earL`, `earR`) behind the head, a lop's floppy ones (`flapL`, `flapR`) over it, then `crown` over those for the hat and anything the coat draws over the ears (a lop's shine and blush); a rabbit's `nose` too. Ears that don't move (a golden's, a bichon's) stay in the head's art.
- Personas: `deadpan`, `energetic`, `showoff`, `drama`, `shy`. Every look plays every persona. Adding one is a new `clips/<name>/` folder with its three clips, plus a `Persona` case of that name; the tests run over every case and check it binds, loops seamlessly, lands where its idle starts and stays on the canvas.
- SVG: flat paths, circles, ellipses and rects with hex fills and strokes. No transforms, gradients, filters, text or arcs (outline them).
- `moves/flat.json`: 躺平, a 请假条 day on the 战报 (`PlaceholderCharacter.lyingFlat(persona:)`): falls flat on its back under gravity (a quarter turn, like the poked 戏精), breathes with its eyes shut and stays down; down from `lyingFlatFrom`, reaching `lyingFlatReach` past either side.
- Downloaded art: admin tools (`put_art`) can put a file at any path here, as `avatar/<path>`, without an app release; the app downloads it into Application Support's `Art/current/avatar/` and reads it there before this folder (`ArtTree`), so a new variant is a new folder there too. Only data the parser draws (the SVG above, JSON, PNG), never code; art that needs rig parts a newer build added carries a higher rig version, and older builds leave it out.
