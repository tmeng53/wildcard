Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada and other US states whose laws protect "consumer health data". It covers the part of Wildcard (外卡, the "App") that may count as health data. Our Privacy Policy covers everything else.

1. What we collect and why

Check-ins: that you trained, the sport and minutes, healthy-meal and cheat-meal check-ins, and the text and photos you add. We use them to show your squad's feed, your weekly progress and the Sunday recap, and for the AI features described in the Privacy Policy.

Workouts from Apple Health (only if you connect it and post one): duration, distance, pace or speed, average and highest heart rate, active calories, effort, laps, heart rate during the workout, the trimmed route and the four heart rate zone boundaries. The zones are worked out on your phone from your birthday and your latest resting heart rate from Apple Health. The resting heart rate never leaves your phone; the birthday, if you give it, is kept with your profile, seen only by you, and you can delete it in Settings. We use them only to show the post you chose to make. They are never sent to AI providers.

We never collect weight, sleep, diagnoses, medications or other body metrics.

2. Where it comes from

From you: what you type and pick in the App, and the workouts you choose to post from Apple Health on your phone.

3. Who we share it with

Members of the squads you post to. Our service providers, only to run the App for us:

  • Supabase: database and photo storage.
  • Google Cloud: our servers.
  • Apple: push notifications, which can carry teammates' check-ins and comments.
  • OpenAI (currently used) and Anthropic (alternative), our AI providers, only for members who agreed to AI use: check-in kind, sport, minutes, text and totals, this week's plan and food goals, and a check-in's photos when the AI coach needs them, as listed in section 4 of the Privacy Policy. Never Apple Health data. They may not use it to train their AI models.

We have no affiliates that receive it. We never sell it.

4. Your rights

You can confirm whether we have your consumer health data, see it and the list of everyone we shared it with, delete it, and withdraw your consent. Delete a check-in from the App, or your whole account under Me → Delete account. For anything else, email support@wildcardsquad.com; we reply within 45 days. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and if the appeal is refused, contact your state attorney general.
