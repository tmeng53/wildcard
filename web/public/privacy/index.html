Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what the developer of Wildcard (外卡), based in New York, USA, ("we") collects when you use the Wildcard app (the "App"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English versions say the same thing; if they differ, the English version controls. Version française.

In one line: your check-ins are seen only by your squad; we don't sell your data, show ads or track you across apps; and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the App at any time.

1. What we collect

Account: the Apple user identifier from Sign in with Apple and, if you allow it, your name and email address (the email can be Apple's private relay address). We never receive your Apple password.

Profile: nickname, the character and outfit you pick, your catchphrase, the sports you do, your weekly plan, and your phone's time zone (to work out "today" and "this week").

Check-ins: the kind (trained, healthy meal, honest cheat meal, daily leave note), sport and minutes, text, up to 3 photos, date and time, and, if you add one, the place you tag it with (its name and where it is, never where your phone is), which your teammates see. Photos are uploaded without their location, camera details or other metadata.

Apple Health (only if you connect it): the App only reads your workouts (duration, distance, heart rate, calories, route and the like), so you can post one in a tap. Only the numbers and map you post leave your phone: you can strike out any number, and the first and last 200 m of a route are cut off. Your resting heart rate stays on your phone, where it sets your heart rate zones with your birthday. The App never reads weight or sleep, and never writes to Apple Health.

Approximate location (only if you allow it): so the AI coach knows the weather where you are, the App sends us your approximate location, to about 11 km, and the name of your city. We keep only the latest one. Your teammates never see it.

Places you tag (only if you add one): to list places near you, the App asks iOS for your precise location for that one time when you tap Add place, and uses it to search Apple Maps. It is never sent to us. We receive only the place you pick.

Birthday (optional, only if you give it): the year, month and day you enter at sign-up or in Me → Settings → Birthday. We use it to work out heart rate zones on your phone. Squadmates can't see your birthday. You can change or delete it in Settings at any time.

Squads and interactions: squads you create or join, invite codes, and your emoji reactions to teammates' check-ins.

AI: when you choose to have AI draft a leave note, the reason you type and the drafts. For the squad's Sunday awards and recap, and for the AI coach if your squad hires one, what members who agreed to AI use post in that squad: nicknames, check-ins (text, sport and minutes, place names), comments, reactions, check-in totals, weekly titles, this week's plan and food goals, and a check-in's photos when the coach needs to see them. If they allowed approximate location, the coach is also told the weather in their city, never the location itself. Workout data from Apple Health is never sent to AI providers.

Push: if you turn on notifications, the device identifier we send them to, and your notification settings.

Technical logs: IP address and time of access, for security and fixing problems.

Crash and performance reports: when the App or our service hits an error, it sends a report: what went wrong, the device model and version numbers, and a random identifier. These reports are not linked to your account and never include your check-ins, photos or screenshots.

Feedback: what you send from the App (Me → Settings → Feedback): your words, up to 3 screenshots, the app and iOS version, device model, app language and your account ID. Only our team sees it (see section 4), to fix what you report. When you tell the AI coach under a post about something you want in the App or something that doesn't work, it may pass on your words, a one-line summary and your account ID. Your name and email are not included.

We do not collect weight or other body metrics (the only calories we receive are a workout's active calories, if you keep them on a post), and we do not read your contacts, and we never receive your precise location: the only locations we receive are your approximate location if you allow it, the places you tag posts with, and the trimmed route of a workout you post with its map, and the only Apple Health data is what you post, as described above. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so we only receive the photos you select. The App has no ads and no third-party analytics. Our website counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies and without tracking you across sites; it records the page, referrer, country and browser type.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App: sign-in, saving check-ins, showing your squad's feed, weekly progress and recaps, and sending notifications you turned on.
  • To draft AI leave notes when you ask for one, to write your squad's weekly awards and recap every Sunday, and, when your squad hires an AI coach, for the coach to reply to check-ins and comments, write its daily tactics line and each member's morning note, and remember lasting things about the squad (such as nicknames and habits).
  • For safety: preventing abuse, handling reports, fixing problems.
  • To contact you about the service only (for example, changes to these terms). No marketing email.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, build advertising profiles, or use your content to train AI models. We will ask for your consent before using it for any purpose not described here.

3. Who can see your content

Members of your squad see your nickname, character, the check-ins and photos you post to that squad (with the workout numbers you keep on them, such as heart rate, and the route map if you keep it), and your weekly progress. A squad has at most 8 members.

People outside your squad cannot. The App has no public profiles. If you save or share a weekly poster to another platform yourself, that platform's rules apply to it.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored in the United States. If you are in Canada or elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States, where courts, law enforcement and national security authorities may be able to access it under US law.

6. Retention and deletion

While your account exists we keep your content; you can delete individual check-ins at any time.

Deleting your account: start it in the App under Me → Delete account (注销账号) and confirm again with Apple. It takes effect at once: we delete your account, profile, check-ins and their workout data, photos, squad memberships, reactions and AI consent record, and revoke the link to your Apple ID. Teammates stop seeing anything of yours. Routine database backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days at most. If the law requires us to keep any record, we keep only that and tell you what was kept.

Technical logs are kept for at most 30 days, and crash and performance reports for at most 90 days.

Feedback you sent stays in our issue tracker after you delete your account, without your name or email, so we can still fix what it reported. Email us and we will remove it.

Your approximate location and its city are replaced each time a new one is sent, and deleted when you turn location off for the App (if you turn it off in iOS Settings, the next time you open the App) or delete your account.

A squad you created stays if it has other members, but your content in it is deleted.

7. Your rights and choices

Access, correction and deletion: most of it can be done in the App; you can also email support@wildcardsquad.com for a copy of your data. We reply within 30 days (45 days for California residents).

Withdrawing consent: turn off notifications, photo access or Apple Health reading in system Settings (Health → Data Access & Devices → Wildcard), strike numbers or the map off a post before you post it, skip AI leave notes (write your own or roll the dice instead), or delete your account. Consent to AI processing (the weekly awards, AI leave notes and the AI coach) covers your whole account and cannot be withdrawn on its own; if you no longer agree, delete your account and we stop sending and delete your data as described in section 6. To stop sharing approximate location, turn it off under Me → Settings → Location, or in system Settings (Privacy & Security → Location Services → Wildcard).

California residents have the right to know the categories of personal information we collect, their sources and purposes; to delete and correct it; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information.

Canadian residents may access and correct their information, withdraw consent, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of protecting personal information and can be reached at support@wildcardsquad.com.

Residents of Singapore, Australia and New Zealand: you may ask to see and correct your information by emailing support@wildcardsquad.com; our Privacy Officer answers. Your information is processed in the United States (section 5) by providers bound by contract to protect it. You may complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or New Zealand's Privacy Commissioner.

Taiwan residents: we use your information for the purposes in section 2, while your account exists (section 6), in the United States and wherever the providers in section 4 process it, and only through those providers. Under the Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to see it, get a copy, correct it, have us stop collecting or using it, or delete it, by emailing support@wildcardsquad.com. Without an account and the data needed to post check-ins, the App can't work; everything else is optional.

8. Consumer health data

In some states (for example Washington and Nevada), workout and meal check-ins, including heart rate from Apple Health, may count as "consumer health data". We collect and use it only for the features above, show it only in the squads you choose, share it only with the providers in section 4, and never sell it. You can access and delete it and withdraw your consent. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy covers it in full.

9. Age

The App is for adults: you must be 18 or older to sign up, and it is not directed to anyone younger. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18 we delete it promptly. Parents can reach us at support@wildcardsquad.com.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Data and photos are stored privately, and only people allowed to see them can. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in the App at least 14 days before they take effect, and ask for consent again for any new purpose.
