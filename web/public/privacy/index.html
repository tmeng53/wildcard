隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日

本政策说明外卡（Wildcard，“本应用”）的开发者（个人，以下称“我们”）在你使用本应用时收集哪些信息、怎么用、和谁共享，以及你有哪些选择。中英文版本意思一致；如有不同，以英文版为准。

一句话版本：你的打卡只给你小队里的人看；我们不卖你的数据、不放广告、不跨 App 追踪你；你随时可以在 App 里删掉账号和全部内容。

1. 我们收集的信息

账号信息：通过“通过 Apple 登录”得到的 Apple 用户标识，以及你的姓名和邮箱（如果你允许；邮箱可以是 Apple 提供的隐藏转发邮箱）。我们不会拿到你的 Apple 密码。

资料：昵称、你选的角色形象和搭配、口头禅、常练的运动、每周计划、手机时区（用来算“今天”和“本周”）。

打卡内容：类型（练了、健康餐、坦白放纵餐、今日请假条）、运动项目和时长、文字、最多 3 张照片、日期和时间。

小队和互动：你加入或创建的小队、邀请码、你对队友打卡的表情回应和对请假条的回复。

AI 相关：当你选择让 AI 帮你写请假条时，你输入的理由和生成的候选文案。每周日为小队写颁奖词和周战报时，小队里同意使用 AI 的成员这一周的打卡文字和请假条内容（不含照片）。

推送：如果你开启通知，设备的推送令牌，以及你的通知开关设置。

技术日志：服务器在处理请求时记录的 IP 地址、时间、请求结果和错误信息，用于安全和排障。

我们不收集体重、热量等身体指标；不读取你的通讯录、位置或健康 App 数据。选照片时使用系统相册选择器，只拿到你选中的那几张。我们目前不使用第三方分析或广告 SDK。

2. 我们怎么用

  • 提供功能：登录、保存打卡、给小队展示动态、算本周进度、生成周战报、发送你开启的通知。
  • 在你点击时生成 AI 请假条；每周日为小队写 AI 颁奖词和周战报。
  • 安全：防滥用、处理举报、排查故障。
  • 联系你：只发与服务相关的必要通知（例如条款变更），不发营销邮件。

我们不卖你的个人信息，不为跨情境广告而“共享”它，不用它建立广告画像，也不用你的内容训练 AI 模型。若要按本政策未写的新目的使用，会先征得你的同意。

3. 谁能看到你的内容

你的小队成员能看到你的昵称、角色、你发到该小队的打卡和照片、本周进度。小队最多 8 人。

不在小队里的人看不到。本应用没有公开主页。如果你自己把周海报保存或分享到其他平台，那部分受该平台的规则管理。

4. 为我们提供服务的第三方

我们只把完成对应功能所需的信息交给下列服务商，它们只能代表我们处理数据。

服务商用途涉及的信息
Apple登录、推送通知Apple 用户标识、姓名和邮箱、推送令牌和通知内容
Supabase数据库、登录会话、照片存储上述全部账号、资料、打卡和照片

AI 服务商只收到文字，用来生成回复。换用其他 AI 服务商或增加用途之前，我们会重新征求你的同意。此外，只有在法律要求、保护用户或公众安全、或业务被并购（并继续受本政策约束）时，我们才会披露信息。

5. 数据存在哪里

数据存放在美国。如果你在加拿大或其他国家，你的信息会被传输到美国并在那里处理，可能被美国法院、执法和国家安全机构依当地法律调取。

6. 保留和删除

账号存续期间，我们保留你的内容；你可以随时删除单条打卡。

删除账号：在 App 里的「我 → 注销账号」发起，并用 Apple 再次确认。删除立即生效：我们会删除你的账号、资料、打卡、照片、小队成员关系、表情回应和 AI 同意记录，并撤销与 Apple ID 的登录关联。队友不再看到你的任何内容。数据库的例行备份（如有）会在最多 30 天内被覆盖。法律要求保留的记录（如有）除外，并会告诉你保留了什么。

技术日志最多保留 30 天。

如果你创建的小队还有其他成员，小队本身保留，但你的内容会被删掉。

7. 你的权利和选择

查看、更正和删除：大部分可以直接在 App 里做；也可以发邮件到 support@wildcardsquad.com 索取你的数据副本。我们在 30 天内答复（加州居民为 45 天）。

撤回同意：可以在系统设置里关闭通知和相册权限；可以不用 AI 请假条（自己写或摇骰子）；可以随时删除账号。对 AI 处理（每周颁奖和 AI 请假条）的同意适用于整个账号，不能单独撤回；如果不再同意，请删除账号，我们会停止发送并按第 6 节删除你的数据。

加州居民：你有权知道我们收集了哪些类别的个人信息及其来源和用途，有权删除、更正，并不因行使权利而受差别对待。我们不出售也不“共享”个人信息。

加拿大居民：你有权查阅和更正个人信息、撤回同意，并可向加拿大隐私专员办公室或魁北克信息委员会投诉。

8. 消费者健康数据

运动和饮食打卡在某些州（如华盛顿州、内华达州）可能属于“消费者健康数据”。我们只为上述功能收集和使用它，只在你选择的小队内展示，只交给第 4 节列出的服务商，绝不出售。你可以查看、删除它，并撤回同意。

9. 未成年人

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不允许他们注册。如果我们得知某个账号属于 13 岁以下的儿童，会尽快删除。家长可以通过 support@wildcardsquad.com 联系我们。

10. 安全

传输全程加密；数据库不对 App 直接开放，只能经我们的服务器按权限访问；照片存在非公开存储中，通过短时上传和下载链接访问。没有绝对安全的系统；发生可能造成严重危害的数据泄露时，我们会按法律要求通知你和监管机构。

11. 政策变更

重大变更会在生效前至少 14 天在 App 内告知。涉及新的使用目的时会重新征求同意。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what the individual developer of Wildcard (外卡) ("we") collects when you use the Wildcard app (the "App"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The Chinese and English versions say the same thing; if they differ, the English version controls.

In one line: your check-ins are seen only by your squad; we don't sell your data, show ads or track you across apps; and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the App at any time.

1. What we collect

Account: the Apple user identifier from Sign in with Apple and, if you allow it, your name and email address (the email can be Apple's private relay address). We never receive your Apple password.

Profile: nickname, the character and outfit you pick, your catchphrase, the sports you do, your weekly plan, and your phone's time zone (to work out "today" and "this week").

Check-ins: the kind (trained, healthy meal, honest cheat meal, daily leave note), sport and minutes, text, up to 3 photos, and date and time.

Squads and interactions: squads you create or join, invite codes, your emoji reactions to teammates' check-ins and your replies to leave notes.

AI: when you choose to have AI draft a leave note, the reason you type and the drafts generated. Every Sunday, to write the squad's awards and weekly recap, the check-in captions and leave-note text (never photos) of squad members who agreed to AI use for that week.

Push: your device's push token if you turn on notifications, and your notification switches.

Technical logs: IP address, time, request outcome and errors, recorded by our servers for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect weight, calories or other body metrics, and we do not read your contacts, location or Health app data. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so we only receive the photos you select. We currently use no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App: sign-in, saving check-ins, showing your squad's feed, weekly progress and recaps, and sending notifications you turned on.
  • To draft AI leave notes when you ask for one, and to write your squad's weekly awards and recap every Sunday.
  • For safety: preventing abuse, handling reports, fixing problems.
  • To contact you about the service only (for example, changes to these terms). No marketing email.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, build advertising profiles, or use your content to train AI models. We will ask for your consent before using it for any purpose not described here.

3. Who can see your content

Members of your squad see your nickname, character, the check-ins and photos you post to that squad, and your weekly progress. A squad has at most 8 members.

People outside your squad cannot. The App has no public profiles. If you save or share a weekly poster to another platform yourself, that platform's rules apply to it.

4. Service providers

We share only what each function needs with the providers below, which process data only on our behalf.

Apart from these providers, we disclose information only when the law requires it, to protect users or the public, or in a merger or acquisition where this policy keeps applying.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored in the United States. If you are in Canada or elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States, where courts, law enforcement and national security authorities may be able to access it under US law.

6. Retention and deletion

While your account exists we keep your content; you can delete individual check-ins at any time.

Deleting your account: start it in the App under Me → Delete account (注销账号) and confirm again with Apple. It takes effect at once: we delete your account, profile, check-ins, photos, squad memberships, reactions and AI consent record, and revoke the link to your Apple ID. Teammates stop seeing anything of yours. Routine database backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days at most. If the law requires us to keep any record, we keep only that and tell you what was kept.

Technical logs are kept for at most 30 days.

A squad you created stays if it has other members, but your content in it is deleted.

7. Your rights and choices

Access, correction and deletion: most of it can be done in the App; you can also email support@wildcardsquad.com for a copy of your data. We reply within 30 days (45 days for California residents).

Withdrawing consent: turn off notifications or photo access in system Settings, skip AI leave notes (write your own or roll the dice instead), or delete your account. Consent to AI processing (the weekly awards and AI leave notes) covers your whole account and cannot be withdrawn on its own; if you no longer agree, delete your account and we stop sending and delete your data as described in section 6.

California residents have the right to know the categories of personal information we collect, their sources and purposes; to delete and correct it; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information.

Canadian residents may access and correct their information, withdraw consent, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

8. Consumer health data

In some states (for example Washington and Nevada), workout and meal check-ins may count as "consumer health data". We collect and use it only for the features above, show it only in the squads you choose, share it only with the providers in section 4, and never sell it. You can access and delete it and withdraw your consent.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and they may not sign up. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13 we delete it promptly. Parents can reach us at support@wildcardsquad.com.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. The App cannot reach the database directly; only our servers can, and they check permissions on every request. Photos sit in private storage and are reached through short-lived upload and download links. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in the App at least 14 days before they take effect, and ask for consent again for any new purpose.
